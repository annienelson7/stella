How to Know What to Book

Every client's hair is different — here's a quick breakdown to help you find the service that fits your vibe and your goals.

Dramatic & Uniform

Full highlights provide a more dramatic, uniform change by lightening the hair throughout or adding contrast. This technique is ideal for clients looking for an overall brighter appearance or transitioning to a significantly lighter shade.

Subtle & Budget-Friendly

Partial highlights are great for adding brightness, dimension, or soft contrast without committing to a full head of color. They are also faster and more budget-friendly than full highlights.

This technique is ideal for subtle changes, face-framing effects, or maintaining a natural base color. It's also a great option between full highlight services to maintain brightness.

Quick Maintenance

A mini foil service uses a small number of foils, usually around the hairline, part line, or crown, to add a touch of brightness or refresh existing color.

This service is often used for quick maintenance between full or partial highlight appointments, to subtly frame the face, or to blend out new growth.

Lived-In & Low Maintenance

Lightener is hand-painted onto the hair in a sweeping motion to create a natural, sun-kissed look. This technique creates a low-maintenance style because it grows out more subtly, with no harsh lines or obvious regrowth.

It's ideal for those who want dimension, brightness, and an effortless, lived-in look.

Corrective & Bold

A corrective color service designed to fix, adjust, or completely transform previous color work.

All-Over Color

An all-over color service using demi-permanent or permanent color.

Starting at 80g of color.
